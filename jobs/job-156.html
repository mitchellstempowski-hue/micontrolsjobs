Automation & Controls Technician

RWC, Inc. · Bay City, Michigan

Type: Full-time

RWC, Inc., a global leader in the design and production of innovative manufacturing systems is accepting resumes for an Automation & Controls Technician. We're looking for a hands-on Automation & Controls Technician to troubleshoot, test, and start up custom automated equipment in our shop and at customer facilities. This is a full-time role located in Bay City, MI, for an Automation & Controls Technician. The Automation & Controls Technician will be responsible for troubleshooting and resolving automation issues, performing preventive maintenance tasks, and ensuring automation systems operate according to specifications. Travel to customer sites is required. Essential Functions: Troubleshoot and resolve automation issues, including electrical, mechanical, pneumatic, and fluid circuitry. Program and troubleshoot Rockwell PLC systems. Participate in machine setup, testing, and debugging according to project specifications and test plans. Conduct startup service, repairs, and training at customer sites. Essential Qualifications: Hands-on experience troubleshooting and programming PLC-controlled automation systems. Experience with Rockwell PLC platform required.
