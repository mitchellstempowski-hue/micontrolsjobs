Controls Engineer, PLC

Schenck USA Corp · Southfield, Michigan

Pay: $125,000 - $135,000 a year

Join Our Team as a Controls Engineer! Ready to engineer the future of automation? We're seeking a Controls Engineer who thrives on designing, programming, and optimizing advanced machinery and equipment. Apply today and grow your career with Schenck USA! What We Offer: Competitive benefits package. Ongoing training and career development opportunities. Employee recognition through our Service Award Points program. Engaging team events and social activities. Why Work for Schenck USA? What the ideal candidate will do: Design electrical control systems and create schematics for custom automation equipment using AutoCAD. Program, modify, and troubleshoot PLC and HMI systems using Allen-Bradley and Siemens platforms. Develop and maintain controls documentation, bills of materials (BOMs), and project records. Manage controls engineering activities to support project schedules, budgets, and performance goals. Review customer requirements, specifications, and drawings to ensure successful project execution. Develop functional specifications and support system testing, commissioning, and customer acceptance activities. Collaborate with other departments and team members. Provide technical support for project quoting, system design, and new equipment development. Assist with equipment installation, startup, troubleshooting, training, and field service as needed. Identify and implement control system improvements, standardization efforts, and new technologies. Must be a U.S. person. Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering or related engineering field. Minimum of 5 years of hands-on controls engineering experience in industrial automation, custom machinery, machine design, or test equipment manufacturing. Proficiency with PLC programming and troubleshooting, including Allen-Bradley and Siemens platforms, plus HMI development. Experience designing electrical control systems and schematics using AutoCAD or EPLAN. Strong knowledge of electrical controls, industrial networking (EtherNet/IP, DeviceNet), pneumatics, hydraulics, and machine safety standards. Experience commissioning, troubleshooting, and supporting automated equipment in a manufacturing environment. About Us: Schenck USA Corp. is a German-owned company comprised of several brands and business units: Schenck and Test Devices. Our US headquarters is located in Deer Park, NY with branches in Southfield, MI and Hudson, MA. We specialize in industrial balancing, spin testing, automation, and vibration analysis.
