Controls Technician

APEX Controls, LLC · Hudsonville, Michigan

At APEX Controls, Controls Technicians transform engineering designs into functioning automation systems. The role combines hands-on electrical assembly, panel wiring, troubleshooting, and system testing to help deliver high-quality solutions to customers. It is suited to someone who enjoys working with electrical systems, takes pride in quality workmanship, and wants to develop a career in automation.

Responsibilities include assembling and wiring industrial control panels and equipment; reading and interpreting electrical schematics, layouts, and wiring diagrams; installing and terminating electrical components, devices, and controls; troubleshooting electrical systems and identifying wiring issues; and supporting testing, startup, and commissioning activities. Requirements include experience in electrical controls, industrial maintenance, automation, or a related field, and the ability to read electrical drawings and schematics.
