Off-Shift Automation Technician

Magna · Highland Park, Michigan

What we offer: At Magna, you can expect an engaging and dynamic environment where you can help to develop industry-leading automotive technologies. We invest in our employees, providing them with the support and resources they need to succeed. As a member of our global team, you can expect exciting, varied responsibilities as well as a wide range of development prospects. Group Summary: The Magna Exteriors portfolio of products includes access systems such as liftgates, exterior trim, modular systems, front-end modules including fascia, active aerodynamic systems and other lightweight structural components for automotive, commercial truck and other industrial markets. Job Responsibilities: To follow Company policies and display conduct expected of MEHP employees as described in the employee handbook. Stay current with advancements in automation technology, facility maintenance, and best practices. Be flexible with work schedule to work varying hours due to availability of time to work on machine issues. Design, maintain, and continuously improve all assembly equipment to ensure safe and efficient operation. Review and implement secondary equipment regards to machine operation and new technologies. Support LEAN manufacturing including lean thinker. Support and improve departmental KOI's that include correcting handling damage (HD) causes, reducing costs by eliminating waste, improving PM completion percentage, reducing downtime, and secondary equipment readiness. Support maintenance and engineering activities, including preventative maintenance, troubleshooting and repair of all equipment. Assist automation engineers and other departments. Available to work weekends as required. Worker Type: Regular / Permanent. Group: Magna Exteriors
