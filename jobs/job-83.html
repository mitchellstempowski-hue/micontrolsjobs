Controls Engineer

OpTech LLC · Flint, Michigan

Pay: $58 - $60 an hour · Shift: 8 hour shift

Position Summary: We are seeking an experienced Controls Engineer to support production operations at a the client engine plant. This hands-on, on-site role will focus on troubleshooting, maintaining, and improving automated machinery and control systems in the V8 machine-floor production area. The ideal candidate will have strong expertise in the client Global Common Controls Hardware and Software (GCCSCH), Rockwell and Siemens PLCs, Siemens HMI systems, and FANUC robotics. The Controls Engineer will work closely with Maintenance and Production teams to diagnose equipment issues, minimize downtime, support production continuity, and implement effective control-system solutions. This position requires an individual who can independently troubleshoot and work with existing controls systems with limited foundational training. Key Responsibilities: Provide hands-on controls engineering support for machinery and equipment in the V8 machine-floor production area. Troubleshoot PLCs, HMIs, robotics, and automated manufacturing equipment to minimize downtime and maintain production. Work with the client Global Common Controls Hardware and Software (GCCSCH) standards and systems. Develop, modify, and troubleshoot Rockwell PLC programs and Siemens Step 7 and TIA Portal PLC applications, including TIA Portal Versions 17 and 18. Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Automation, Electrical Engineering, Controls Engineering, or a related technical field preferred but not required. 8-10 years of relevant controls engineering, industrial automation, or manufacturing technology experience preferred. Experience with Siemens TIA Portal Versions 17 and 18 and Siemens HMI systems highly preferred. Work on-site in the the client engine plant, primarily supporting the V8 machine-floor production area.
