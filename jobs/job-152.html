Controls Engineer

General Motors · Warren, Michigan

We are seeking an experienced Controls Engineer specializing in industrial automation and manufacturing controls to support the design, implementation, launch, and ongoing support of automated manufacturing systems. This position will focus heavily on machining operations and controls systems, requiring strong experience with Siemens automation technologies, including TIA Portal. This role requires extensive travel to manufacturing facilities during critical project execution and launch phases. Program, troubleshoot, and maintain Siemens PLC, HMI, and related automation systems. Experience programming, commissioning, and troubleshooting industrial PLC-based control systems. Experience integrating PLCs, HMIs, industrial networks, drives, robotics, and machine control systems. Deep expertise with Siemens PLCs, TIA Portal, and Step 7. This role is categorized as hybrid. This means the selected candidate is expected to report to a specific location at least 3 times a week {or other frequency dictated by their manager}. The selected candidate will be required to travel at least 50% or more on a frequent basis.
