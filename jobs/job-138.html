Strategic Accounts Controls Specialist

Johnson Controls · Madison Heights, Michigan

Pay: $38 - $52 · Type: Full-time

Johnson Controls is hiring a Strategic Accounts Controls Specialist to program, repair, install and retrofit, commission, maintain, and service building HVAC and control systems. Under minimal supervision, the specialist troubleshoots systems and provides direct, on-site customer support, aiming to resolve service issues and improve building efficiency. The role requires strong technical competence and includes mentoring and training controls, service, and mechanical technicians, as well as advising customers on system operation, enhancements, upgrades, replacements, and service solutions.

The specialist also supports building-control project design and configuration, software development and testing, system commissioning, project coordination, and preparation of technical and close-out documentation. Responsibilities include diagnosing and repairing complex control-system malfunctions, including testing and modifying software in multiple system languages; installing and modifying software and graphics; assisting with loading and commissioning system- and network-level controllers and validating complete system functionality; and training customers and owner/operators in control-system operations.
