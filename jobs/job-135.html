Controls Project Engineer

Dematic · Grand Rapids, Michigan

Pay: $127,504 - $166,000

The base pay range for this role is estimated to be $127,504 - $166,000 at the time of posting. Final compensation will be determined by various factors such as work location, education, experience, knowledge, and skills.

What You Will Do In This Role:

Lead complex projects through the project lifecycle, including delegating project tasks as appropriate.

Design complex material handling systems that integrate both software and hardware components.

Perform the full range of standard work for all subsystems on any given material handing project.

Train and develop lower-level engineers by providing detailed instructions and mentoring for other team members.

Understand interface between Dematic system and external 3rd party systems.

Troubleshoot complex programming and electrical issues that arise on a customer site.

Commission critical path work independently as well as lead on-site commissioning teams for large projects.

Participate in schedule review, aligning ME/CE segments.

Compare sales/estimating documents with company and customer standards. Schedule and facilitate Hardware, Software, Safety and System Tech reviews.

Develop staffing plan and implementation plan. Track project financials. Check and approve all system drawings.

Verify PLC code and emulation. Document, program, and test host interfaces.

Verify final commissioning plan from PM for concerns. Lead commissioning efforts on-site. Assist other engineers in hardware and software troubleshooting techniques.

Requires a bachelor's degree in mechatronics engineering, electrical engineering or closely related field and 4 years of experience as a Commissioning Engineer, Controls Engineer, Electrical Engineer or closely related occupation.

Also requires 2 years of experience with Merges: Zipper Merges, Time-Based Merges, Encoder-Based Merges, and Side Induct Merges; Sorters: PLC Sorters, RATS (Right Angle Transfers), SWD (Steerable Wheel Diverts), Flexsort Controlled Sorters (SL2, Rs200), and Gapper; Distributed Controls Architecture: Motorized Driven Roller Controllers, ECCs (Embedded Conveyor Controllers), DMCs (Dual Motor Controllers), Asi-Safe through ASIMON, AB PLCs, and AB VFDs; and Software: Maestro, Emulate 3D, RS Logix, and Siemens TIA Portal.

50% travel required to various unanticipated locations primarily in the U.S. Located at Dematic Corp. Headquarters in Grand Rapids, MI. Telecommuting permitted from anywhere in the U.S.
