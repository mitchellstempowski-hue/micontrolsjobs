Controls Engineering Supervisor

Eaton · Grand Rapids, Michigan

Pay: $97,000 - $143,000 per year · Type: Full-time

Eaton's IS AER FMC division is currently seeking a Controls Engineering Supervisor. This is a Full Time (M-F Daylight), onsite role based at our Aerospace Manufacturing plant in Grand Rapids, MI!

The expected annual salary range for this role is $97000 - $143000 a year.

What you'll do:

The Controls Engineering Supervisor is responsible for driving technical and process excellence while providing strong people leadership and delivering timely results at Eaton's Grand Rapids site. This role leads automation and test equipment initiatives supporting electro-mechanical actuator products, including actuators, controllers, motors, locking mechanisms and related components. The position balances hands-on technical contribution with supervisory responsibility, ensuring production test systems are robust, compliant, and aligned with manufacturing readiness goals.

Job Responsibilities:

Provide controls engineering / automation leadership for improved Manufacturing Readiness Levels for new products

Provide hands-on test leadership for all production test equipment at the site

Manage the design, build, and verification of production test equipment through external and internal suppliers for Capital projects.

Ensure test equipment update requirements from Manufacturing Change Orders are implemented in a timely manner.

Promote Continuous Improvement mindset with testing plans and test equipment consolidation ideas in line with developing a 5-yr CAPEX plan.

Create and release drawings and schematics as updates are needed.

Perform troubleshooting and repair of test equipment.

Perform hands-on testing as required

Comply with Eaton's high expectations for a safe work environment.

Qualifications — Required (Basic) Qualifications:

Bachelor's degree in engineering or computer science fields from an accredited institution

5+ years of experience within a manufacturing environment

3+ years of experience in manufacturing, sustaining, and/or test engineering

Must be able to work in the United States without corporate sponsorship now and within the future
