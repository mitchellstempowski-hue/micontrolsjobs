Controls Design Engineer

Insight Global · Lansing Charter Township, Michigan

Job Description

Insight Global is seeking a Controls Design Engineer for our client, a small, privately owned designer and manufacturer of microfinishing solutions for the automotive industry. This role involves the design, programming, and analysis of machine control systems from initial concept through production, startup, and customer acceptance.

Key Responsibilities

Lead the design of machine control systems that meet customer specifications while minimizing cost and engineering time, leveraging existing designs and programs whenever possible

Design control component layouts that meet applicable standards and specifications, emphasizing safety, reliability, simplicity, efficient use of space, and cost effectiveness

Program machine controllers to meet operational, performance, and safety requirements

Evaluate internal and external design capabilities and provide accurate documentation to ensure projects remain on schedule and within customer specifications

Coordinate and lead control design reviews with the Mechanical Project Engineer and assigned project coordinators prior to machine build

Prepare and submit required documentation to customers according to project schedules for approval

Maintain organized project records, including purchase orders, drawing approvals, and related documentation

Obtain competitive quotations for electrical control components (e.g., enclosures, pushbutton stations), issue requisitions, and manage long-lead-time items to support project timelines

Collaborate with shop personnel during machine build, startup, debugging, and run-off activities

Support service technicians and training personnel during machine startup at customer facilities

Provide technical support to the Sales and Parts departments as needed

Attend meetings related to project scope, scheduling, and progress

Stay current with emerging industry technologies and advise the Engineering Manager on innovations that could be incorporated into future machine designs

Skills and Requirements

5-7 years of experience in designing electrical control systems for industrial machines

Experience programming Allen-Bradley PLCs, Siemens PLCs, and Fanuc PNCs

Experience with EPLAN and/or AutoCAD Electrical

Strong analytical skills, with the ability to compile and interpret data, recognize patterns, draw logical conclusions, and make sound recommendations

Bachelor's or Master's degree in Controls Engineering or a related field

Some domestic travel may be needed (5%) - Experience with internal combustion engines or powertrain applications is a plus
