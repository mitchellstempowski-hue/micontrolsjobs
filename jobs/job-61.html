Health, Dental, Vision 401K Short term and long-term disability and FSA PTO / Sick Time Paid holidays Paid time off (PTO) Job Details Lead PLC Controls Technician - Data Centers Location: Remote / Nationwide (Must be willing to travel 95%) Industry: Mission Critical / Data Centers / Industrial Automation Employment Type: Full-Time About the Opportunity: We're looking for an experienced Lead PLC Controls Technician to oversee field execution, startup, and commissioning of advanced automation systems within mission-critical data center environments. This is a hands-on leadership position responsible for troubleshooting complex PLC systems, coordinating field technicians, and ensuring successful integration of PLC, SCADA/HMI, and industrial control networks. Candidates can live anywhere in the U.S., provided they're comfortable with approximately 95% nationwide travel. Company-paid travel, lodging, and per diem are included. Key Responsibilities Lead field technicians through PLC installation verification, startup, commissioning, testing, and system turnover. Troubleshoot PLC programming, I/O, communication networks, control sequences, and system integration issues. Support SCADA/HMI integration, processor redundancy, network validation, and failover testing. Review PLC programs, electrical drawings, network diagrams, and commissioning documentation. Coordinate with engineers, project managers, contractors, and commissioning teams to resolve technical challenges. Oversee field quality, safety compliance, testing documentation, and project closeout. Mentor technicians and provide technical leadership throughout project execution. Qualifications 5+ years of experience with PLC controls, industrial automation, or related control systems. Strong experience with Allen-Bradley/Rockwell Studio 5000, FactoryTalk, Siemens TIA Portal, or similar PLC platforms. Ability to troubleshoot ladder logic, I/O systems, industrial networks, and PLC communications. Familiarity with SCADA/HMI systems and protocols such as EtherNet/IP, Modbus, BACnet, and OPC UA. Experience with startup, commissioning, functional testing, and system troubleshooting. Previous experience leading technicians or coordinating field activities. Data center or other mission-critical project experience strongly preferred. OSHA 10 certification or willingness to complete company-provided training. Ability to travel nationwide approximately 95%, including occasional weekends and after-hours work. Benefits & Perks 100% employer-paid individual medical insurance, plus dental and vision coverage 401(k) with company match Flexible PTO Company-paid travel, lodging, and per diem Phone allowance, tools, and PPE
