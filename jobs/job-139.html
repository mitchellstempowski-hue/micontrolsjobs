Electrical Controls Engineer

Abbott · Sturgis, Michigan

Pay: $61,300.00 – $122,700.00 · Type: Full-time

As an Electrical Controls Engineer, you will support manufacturing operations by troubleshooting, maintaining, and improving plant electrical and automation systems. The role focuses on equipment reliability, electrical troubleshooting, controls support, and continuous improvement initiatives that improve safety, quality, and production performance. This position works out of Abbott's Sturgis, MI location in the Abbott Nutrition division.

Responsibilities include designing, implementing, and maintaining electrical systems, control systems, and automation solutions for plant operations; supporting installation, startup, and commissioning of manufacturing equipment and electrical upgrades; troubleshooting and supporting plant electrical systems, including motor control centers (MCCs), variable frequency drives (VFDs), sensors, instrumentation, safety circuits, and control panels; and programming and supporting PLCs (Allen Bradley, Modicon), SCADA systems (GE IFIX), HMI platforms (FactoryTalk, Ignition), and data historians (OSIsoft).

Required qualifications include a Bachelor's degree in engineering (Electrical, Computer, Chemical, or related field) or equivalent work experience, and 2+ years of engineering, automation, or project management experience. The base pay for this position is $61,300.00 – $122,700.00.
