Job Summary

Location: River Rouge, MI

This role supports cokemaking processes by engaging with plant personnel in the field and in the office to support ongoing operational and maintenance activities, identify and manage projects to improve operations, support response to emergent issues, and facilitate internal and external company communications as a collaborative member of the management team.

Key Accountabilities

Support maintenance activities conducted by EES electricians, EES electrical and instrument technicians, and third-party resources. This includes preventative maintenance, corrective maintenance, project work, and developing reliability strategies

Assist in mentoring and training of electricians, electrical and instrument technicians, and other related personnel on equipment functionality, control schemes, and troubleshooting practices

Facilitate PLC maintenance throughout the plant to ensure reliable equipment performance

Facilitate DCS functionality throughout the plant, creating/updating control schemes, overseeing software updates, and implementing cybersecurity with internal and external resources

Maintain systems used for environmental compliance including CEMS and COMS

Coordinate redistribution of plant power in case of supply failure or required maintenance of related infrastructure

Coordinate with Electrical Engineer on equipment procurement, maintenance strategies, and project management

Coordinate with IT resources on maintenance of data historian

Coordinate with IT resources on maintenance of plant communications (internet, phones, radios, etc.)

Minimum Education & Experience Requirements

Bachelor's degree in electrical engineering or electrical engineering technology, or related technical field and a minimum of four (4) years of related engineering experience; or

Associate degree in Electrical Engineering, Electrical Engineering Technology, or related technical field and a minimum of six (6) years of related engineering experience

Experience is preferably obtained in a coke plant, chemical or petrochemical manufacturing facility, or other heavy industrial setting
