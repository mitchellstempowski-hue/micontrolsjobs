Automation Technician

Means Industries · Shelby Charter Township, Michigan

As an Automation Technician at Amsted Automotive, you will support the setup, troubleshooting, and operation of robotic and automated equipment throughout the manufacturing facility. Working alongside Maintenance Technicians, you will help resolve equipment issues, perform preventive maintenance, support new processes, and optimize automation for production and quality. The role provides hands-on experience with robotics and manufacturing automation while building technical skills.

Responsibilities include assisting with the setup, operation, and troubleshooting of robotic and automated equipment; monitoring production equipment for robotic faults and performing basic troubleshooting, resets, and restarts; and supporting preventive maintenance activities for robotics and automation equipment. Requirements include experience working in a manufacturing environment, experience working with Nachi and FANUC robots, and basic PLC knowledge, including Allen-Bradley and Logix software.
