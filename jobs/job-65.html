PLC Controls Engineer

Leidos · Walled Lake, Michigan

Pay: $67,600.00 - $122,200.00

Leidos is seeking an experienced PLC Controls Engineer for work at its Walled Lake, Michigan office. We are seeking qualified individuals with experience to excel in a high-performance team responsible for the development and delivery of market leading controls services and solutions.

Primary Responsibilities: As a Controls Engineer, you will provide technical expertise and work as a part of a team with other engineers and consultants involved in the design and implementation of diverse and challenging solutions for industrial and commercial clients. The ideal candidate would be responsible for the entire lifecycle of a project. Your responsibilities will range from performing system requirements analysis, hardware design, PLC programming, integration, verification and commissioning of controls systems.

Basic Qualifications: Bachelor's degree with two plus (2+) years of relevant experience. Experience with Allen-Bradley, Siemens, and/or Schneider PLC/HMI Programming. Controls programming and troubleshooting skills. Ability to read and interpret blueprints, wiring diagrams, and device data sheets. Travel up to 50% of the time.
