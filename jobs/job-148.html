Controls Design Engineer I

JR Automation · Holland, Michigan

Type: Full-time

JR Automation, a Hitachi Group Company, is hiring a Controls Design Engineer in Holland, Michigan. The role supports the design and execution of custom automation solutions, including electrical and fluid power design, schematics, and component selection. The engineer works with internal and external vendors, customers, and cross-functional teams to ensure equipment meets customer or JR Automation specifications and applicable industry standards. The position is onsite and may involve up to 10% yearly travel for training and customer site visits.

Requirements: Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering or a related field, or 5 years of experience in the automation industry. Proficient use of CAD software, specifically AutoCAD Electrical, and Microsoft Office Suite.

Responsibilities: Interface with internal and external vendors and customers. Conduct electrical and fluid power design. Create electrical and fluid power schematics. Specify electrical and fluid power components for bills of materials.
