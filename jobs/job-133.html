Controls Engineer 2nd Shift

Lear Corporation · Flint, Michigan

As a member of the Auburn Hills team, the Controls Engineer is responsible for developing and implementing program changes to reduce machine downtime and improve system efficiency, while supporting department goals. The role also maintains machinery systems in a just-in-time or complex assembly plant environment. The position is listed for second shift in Flint, Michigan.

Requirements

Bachelor's degree (BAS), three years of related experience and/or training, or an equivalent combination of education and experience.

Required PLC experience with ControlLogix, MicroLogix, and CompactLogix using Rockwell software; ability to understand ladder logic and make programming changes.

Experience programming HMIs, such as PanelView.

Experience with automation equipment, including Fanuc or ABB robots and vision systems.

Experience with electric torque control equipment, such as Atlas Copco or Stanley.

Responsibilities

Develop and implement software and hardware changes to improve efficiency and quality.

Troubleshoot and program PLCs to resolve problems and improve efficiency and quality.

Instruct and coordinate department personnel to maintain high efficiency and productivity.

Coordinate work with outside contractors, vendors, and suppliers.

Work with other departments to optimize production-line performance.

Implement improvement plans and activities.

Maintain accurate records of part-specific parameters and changes.

Requisition tools, equipment, and supplies.

Maintain adequate parts and supplies to minimize production downtime.

Maintain accurate machine and asset records, including repairs, drawings, diagrams, manuals, and preventive maintenance.

Assist with launch programs as necessary.

Mentor and train plant controls engineers and support production as needed.
