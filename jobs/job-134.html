Lead Instrumentation & Controls Engineer 1 - Nuclear

Sargent & Lundy · Ann Arbor, Michigan

Pay: $118,023–$180,313

The Lead Instrumentation & Controls Engineer will work in a project team with engineers and designers to design, modify, and analyze instrumentation and control systems for the Fermi 2 power plant. The role represents Sargent & Lundy at the Fermi 2 site in Newport, Michigan, with occasional travel to the station. Projects involve investigating, troubleshooting, and resolving electrical controls engineering issues, supporting implementation at client facilities, and coordinating with clients and vendors.

The engineer develops digital systems designs, including PLC, DCS, HSI, and networked computer systems; prepares system requirements and procurement specifications; and prepares or reviews technical reports, calculations, studies, diagrams, and drawings. The position also includes managing resources and estimates, supporting proposals, evaluating vendor bids, reviewing vendor documents, verifying equipment compliance, and witnessing factory acceptance tests.

Sargent & Lundy's Nuclear Power Group works on nuclear engineering, design, and licensing, including life extensions and digital modernization of existing plants, advanced reactor technology, small modular reactors, reactor restart projects, coal-to-nuclear initiatives, and international nuclear new-build projects. The company describes its work as supporting clean, carbon-free energy and reducing emissions.

The compensation range is $118,023–$180,313. Eligible positions include a base salary or hourly rate and a comprehensive benefits package; employees may also be eligible for a yearly discretionary bonus. The company does not sponsor U.S. work authorization for this position.
