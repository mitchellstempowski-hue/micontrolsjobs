Controls Engineer

JVIS USA · Benton Harbor, Michigan

SUMMARY: The Controls Engineer will apply knowledge of Controls and Mechanical Technology to new and existing processes to maximize Overall Equipment Effectiveness. Train, coach, and mentor manufacturing and technical personnel. Reports directly to the Maintenance Manager.

ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES:

Assist in the development and design of the controls software packages utilizing Programmable Logic Controllers (PLC) and/or Human/Machine Interfaces (HMI).

Develop tools and documents to troubleshoot problems quickly.

Engineer control hardware / software upgrades necessary to improve operating efficiency of equipment.

Examines and understands specifications in order to ensure the functionality of the equipment meets these specifications.

Modification of process equipment control system to ensure maximum production output while minimizing down-time: this includes modifications to improve plant-wide costs and efficiencies.

Ensures the equipment being started up and commissioned operates in accordance with company requirements.

Programs robotic and/or controls equipment per company specifications.

Performs preventative maintenance as requested by the customer to ensure minimum down-time and optimum operation of machines and equipment.

Train maintenance technicians in equipment operation and troubleshooting.

Maintain specification standards for new and existing process equipment control systems.

Repair and troubleshoot new and existing process equipment control systems.

General machine safety, electrical safety and machine guarding: high level of safety awareness (OSHA, NEC and RIA).

Maintain compliance of IATF 16949 / ISO 9001 / ISO 14001.

Technical Position Qualifications:

Extensive experience programming Allen Bradley/Rockwell and Siemens PLC/HMI software.

Industrial Networks Knowledge. (Ethernet I/P, Device Net, Profibus, ControlNet.)

Auto CAD Electrical experience developing schematics, panel layout, and bill of materials.

Extensive experience with VFD and Servo drive systems.

Thorough knowledge of power distribution as it applies to machine controls.

Knowledgeable in the design and application of fluid power controls. (hydraulic, pneumatic, vacuum).

Thorough knowledge of electro-mechanical control devices. (optical, inductive, capacitive sensors / contactors, relays, switches / power supplies and transformers / motors and motor starters.)

Experience with vision systems.

Read and understand electrical, hydraulic, and pneumatic schematics and prints

EDUCATION And/or EXPERIENCE: Bachelor's Electrical Engineering Degree preferred or extensive experience in Hydraulic Circuits, Blueprint reading, Hydraulic, Pneumatic and Electrical. Minimum 5 years Machine Repair and Maintenance experience.
