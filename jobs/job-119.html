Controls Engineer

Autokiniton · Grand Ledge, Michigan

Shift: 2nd shift

AUTOKINITON delivers operational excellence, high-quality execution, and customer service. We offer 100% vertical integration capabilities such as end-to-end engineering, manufacturing, and in-house logistics. We're proud of our Tier 1 supplier legacy for manufacturing propulsion-agnostic, structural automotive components, and assemblies around the globe. Our core value, “We, not I,” reflects our commitment to collaboration, integrity, and shared success.

To help us drive excellence: As a Controls Engineer, you will be accountable to maintain PLC based controls systems for manufacturing automation and instrumentation, and support Plant floor automation control systems for new and existing equipment.

Support operations in electrical/controls troubleshooting and continuous improvement activities.

Participate in multi-disciplinary teams to enhance continuous improvement, lean manufacturing, and overall production initiatives.

Hands-on knowledge of PLC, ABB, advanced HMI programming, Fanuc and Motorman.

Develop and support motor drive configuration for servo, variable frequency, and DC systems.

Design of electrical, pneumatic, and hydraulic controls systems for new and retrofit equipment.

Work on Plant floor as needed with multi-disciplinary team to troubleshoot to resolve automation issues to maintain/improve production efficiency.

This position reports to the Maintenance Manager.

SUCCESS FACTORS:

Bachelor's degree in Electrical, Controls, Automation or Mechatronics Engineering. Open for equivalent years of experience as Controls Engineer.

5+ years of experience as Controls Engineer in automotive manufacturing; strongly preferred welding assembly facility.

Exceptional knowledge of PLC, ABB, advanced HMI programming, Fanuc and Motorman.

Advanced electrical/mechanical aptitude, working with electrical/controls circuitry and schematics in a manufacturing environment.

Strong problem-solving, self-starter, and thrives in a fast-paced environment.

Excellent organizational, analytical, and communication skills within a multi-disciplinary manufacturing environment.

Continuous Improvement and Lean Manufacturing methodologies are a strong plus.

Advance proficiency with MS OFFICE, ERP, AutoCAD systems. CAD a plus.
