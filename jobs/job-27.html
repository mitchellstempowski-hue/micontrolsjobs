GABLETEK · Marshall, Michigan

FANUC Robot Programmer

Location: Marshall, MI

Contract Length: 4-6+ Weeks

Start Date: ASAP

Employment Type: Contract

Position Overview

We are seeking experienced FANUC Robot Programmers for a 3–6 month contract in Marshall, MI. The ideal candidate works independently with little to no guidance and is comfortable programming, troubleshooting, and recovering FANUC robotics systems in a fast-paced manufacturing environment.

Responsibilities

Program, modify, and optimize FANUC robot programs for production applications

Perform TCP setup and verification, path corrections, position adjustments, and touch-ups

Troubleshoot and recover FANUC robots from faults, alarms, and crashes during live production

Support robotic applications including material handling, welding, and assembly

Perform offline programming and simulation using FANUC Roboguide

Read and interpret electrical schematics and technical documentation

Support machine builds, runoffs, and customer buy-off activities

Maintain accurate documentation of programs, backups, and process changes

Ensure compliance with all safety procedures around automated equipment

Requirements

3+ years of hands-on FANUC robot programming experience — REQUIRED

Ability to write programs independently from scratch

Strong fault recovery and troubleshooting skills

Comfortable working independently with minimal supervision

Ability to read and interpret schematics and technical documentation
