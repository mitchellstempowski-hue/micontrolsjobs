FANUC / Allen-Bradley Robotics & PLC Programmer (Production Support)

Three Rivers, MI | 1 Month Contract

Per diem included for non-local candidates | 1st and 2nd shift available | Ideal Start Date: November 2nd

We’re looking for an experienced programmer who knows both FANUC robotics and Allen-Bradley PLCs to provide production support on a project in Three Rivers, MI. This is a one month contract, and the start date is November 2nd.

What You’ll Do

Provide onsite production support for automated FANUC robotic cells and Allen-Bradley PLC controlled systems

Troubleshoot and resolve robot and PLC faults to minimize downtime

Modify and optimize robot paths and PLC logic as production needs change

Work with production, maintenance, and engineering teams to keep lines running at rate

Support process improvements and document program changes

What We’re Looking For

Hands-on programming experience with both FANUC robots and Allen-Bradley PLCs (RSLogix/Studio 5000)

Strong troubleshooting skills in a live production environment

Ability to read electrical schematics and work safely around automated equipment
