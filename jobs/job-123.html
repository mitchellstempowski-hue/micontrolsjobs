Sr. Controls Engineer - Automation

Dana · Novi, Michigan

Dana is a global leader in the supply of highly engineered driveline, sealing, and thermal-management technologies that improve the efficiency and performance of vehicles with both conventional and alternative-energy powertrains.

Job Purpose: This role will help develop Dana's manufacturing controls architecture, approve the design and type of equipment used in production lines, and help support Dana's global manufacturing strategy. The primary focus of this role will be to ensure the successful launch of capital equipment globally for Dana's new automation and program launches.

Job Duties and Responsibilities:

Implements equipment, automation, and facility strategies to support advanced process development in our manufacturing centers

Leads the development of process technologies required to support long term product technology roadmaps in product and advanced engineering

Initiates benchmarking activities to ensure Dana is assessing state of the art technology for all manufacturing methods and processes

Write and debug PLC logic for proper content, error-proofing, and data collection function

Participates in the advanced development of manufacturing processes for product deployment

Develop/ maintain standard manufacturing / quality documentation for core processes

Maintain and promote a clean, safe, and organized work environment

Support AMR launches at various Dana Facilities

Support customer program launch activity as required

Travel as necessary to support operations globally

Requirements:

Bachelor's Degree in Engineering or Technology preferred

7+ years of experience in manufacturing Controls processes

Experience programming in Siemens and Rockwell

Must understand statistics, as it relates to manufacturing and assembly processes

Low and high-volume production experience

Excellent verbal and written communication skills

Strong project management skills

Preferred: Experience programming in Beckhoff; Experience integrating Cognex vision systems, DC torque tools, programming servos and robots; HMI programming experience; Robot experience ABB, Fanuc
