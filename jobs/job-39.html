Robot Programmer (Experienced)

GABLETEK · Troy, Michigan

Robot Programmer

Location: Local & Travel Opportunities

Position Overview

We are seeking an experienced Robot Programmer with 3+ years of experience to support automation projects in a machine tool and industrial manufacturing environment. This is a hands-on technical role focused on programming, troubleshooting, and optimizing robotic systems across a variety of robot platforms. The ideal candidate works independently, is comfortable on the shop floor, and can hit the ground running with minimal ramp-up time.

Responsibilities

Perform TCP setup, path programming, position adjustments, and touch-ups

Troubleshoot and recover robots from faults and alarms

Support offline programming and simulation

Collaborate with controls and mechanical teams during machine builds and runoffs

Ensure compliance with all safety procedures around automated equipment

Requirements

3+ years of hands-on robot programming experience

Experience programming with one or multiple of these robots: Fanuc

KUKA

ABB

Kawasaki

Yaskawa / Motoman

Comau

Strong troubleshooting and fault recovery skills

Ability to work independently with minimal supervision

Preferred

Vision system integration experience — Keyence, Cognex, Photoneo, Fanuc 3DV/3DL or comparable

Ability to write programs independently from scratch — not just touch-ups and modifications

Integration/Commissioning experience

Offline programming experience

Automotive or machine tool manufacturing background
