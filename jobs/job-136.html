Senior Controls Engineer

Peak Technologies · Flint, Michigan

Type: Full-time

Automation Intelligence, a Peak Technologies company, is seeking a Senior Controls Engineer to design, develop, and implement advanced control systems for automated equipment and processes. The role leads control system architecture, PLC and HMI programming, and integration of industrial automation components. It also provides technical leadership, mentorship to junior engineers, and support for complex troubleshooting, commissioning, and continuous improvement across multiple projects.

The engineer collaborates with project managers and mechanical and electrical teams to deliver projects on time and meet performance requirements. The position supports commissioning, startup, optimization, and field service at customer sites, and requires travel as needed.

Responsibilities include designing and implementing complex control systems, including PLCs, HMIs, motion controllers, and safety systems; programming and debugging PLCs and HMIs using platforms such as Allen-Bradley, Siemens, or equivalent; and troubleshooting and resolving issues related to system logic, hardware, and communications. Requirements include a Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Control Systems, Automation Engineering, or a related field (equivalent technical experience may be considered in place of a formal degree) and 7 years of relevant or transferable experience in controls hardware and logic programming, with proficiency in PLC and HMI programming, such as Allen-Bradley, Siemens, or comparable systems.
