Electrician for Industrial Systems

ATESTEO · East Lansing, Michigan

Type: Full-time

Set your career in motion with ATESTEO. Join the leader in drivetrain testing at our new test center in East Lansing, Michigan!

ATESTEO is growing! We are opening a new test center in Lansing, Michigan.

Your Responsibilities: Install, maintain, troubleshoot, and repair industrial electrical systems and equipment. Support electrical systems associated with drivetrain test cells, dynamometers, drives, instrumentation, and auxiliary equipment. Troubleshoot control panels, PLCs, HMIs, motor controls, VFDs, sensors, relays, and other control components. Read and interpret electrical schematics, wiring diagrams, control drawings, and technical documentation. Provide electrical and controls support for drivetrain testing, including engines, transmissions, axles, electric motors, and related systems.

Qualifications: 3+ years of experience working with industrial electrical and control systems preferred. Experience with PLC-based control systems and industrial automation.
