Controls Designer

Paslin · Shelby Township, Michigan

Type: Full-time

At Paslin we are all about engineering excellence, innovation and vertical manufacturing competence. Our commitment to quality and excellence is evident in every project we undertake. Our team is passionate about automation powered by innovation!

We're hiring a Controls Designer to develop and improve automation control systems that drive our production processes. If you have hands-on experience with PLC programming, circuit design, and industrial automation, this is your chance to work on impactful projects in a fast-paced environment.

What You'll Do:

You'll design and debug PLC programs using Rockwell or Siemens software, create electrical and fluid circuit diagrams, and ensure systems meet customer specs and safety standards. You'll collaborate with robot programmers, troubleshoot issues, and support installations at customer sites

Skills & Experience:

Degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or related field and/or 2+ years of relevant experience

Strong PLC programming and debugging skills

Experience designing pneumatic, hydraulic, water, and electrical circuits using AutoCAD

Solid understanding of industrial communication networks and control systems

Knowledge of electrical codes and safety standards

Travel: This position requires flexibility to report to multiple Paslin sites. During an installation project this role will require overnight travel to customer sites for periods of up to 12 days at a time over a period of multiple consecutive months. Must have reliable transportation and be able to travel to sites in USA, Canada and Mexico.

Work type & Expected hours of work: Regularly works on-site at local Paslin location, either Hoover, 10-Mile or Cherry Creek. Must be flexible to work at any of these three locations as needed for an extended period of time. Typical work schedule for this role is 6am-230pm, Monday to Friday. During critical phases of the project, work hours may flex up to more than 12 hours per day.
