Electronic Technician

Gerdau · Monroe, Michigan

The Electronic Technician is responsible for troubleshooting, maintaining, repairing, modifying, calibrating, testing, and servicing industrial control systems that support steel production and plant operations. This role works closely with production, maintenance, and electrical teams to ensure the reliability and performance of automated equipment and instrumentation. Key Responsibilities: Install, maintain, troubleshoot, and repair automated industrial control equipment and systems. Utilize diagnostic tools such as digital multimeters, oscilloscopes, data recorders, megohmmeters, and other testing equipment. Support and perform basic programming, configuration, and troubleshooting of Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs) and Human Machine Interfaces (HMIs). Inspect, verify, calibrate, and maintain plant instrumentation and control systems. Collaborate with electricians and maintenance personnel to support equipment installations, upgrades, and repairs. Maintain industrial control system computers, networks, and associated peripherals. Read and interpret electrical drawings, schematics, and technical documentation.
