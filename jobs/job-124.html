Manufacturing Controls Engineer

Nexteer Automotive · Saginaw, Michigan

POSITION: Manufacturing Controls Engineer

LOCATION: Plant 4

MAJOR JOB RESPONSIBILITIES:

Manage and develop machine based PLC logic systems

Manage interface with Nexteer Traceability Systems. Be able to startup, troubleshoot and monitor existing systems

Understand and author permissions based logic between manufacturing cells and rework stations

Assume responsibility for Labview program edits, backups, and Revision control

Be part of the engineering, development and debug of new equipment from controls logic standpoint

Plant Production PC and Manufacturing IT Network support

Utilize project management skills to plan and execute assigned Controls projects

Communicate with all stakeholders, supervisor, engineers, skilled trades, etc. to meet customer requirements

QUALIFICATIONS: DEGREE: BACHELORS MAJOR: ENGINEERING

Knowledge of Ethernet Network Systems (managing and reducing network traffic)

Ability to configure, troubleshoot, and support Manufacturing Floor PCs

Proficient in PLC/HMI program debug and additions

Proficient with Microsoft office tools (Excel, Word, Powerpoint)

Prefer experience multiple programming languages (VB, C, etc)

Prefer experience with Rockwell Programmable Automation Controllers

Prefer experience managing and maintaining SQL Server 2008 and newer databases

Prefer experience with Keyence/Cognex cameras, bar code scanners and printers

Prefer experience with National Instruments Labview software

Prefer experience with Fanuc Robotics programming / troubleshooting

Required to manage multiple projects at one time

High level of analytical skills

High level of interpersonal skills, drive and teamwork
