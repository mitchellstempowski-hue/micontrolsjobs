Controls Engineer

Adient · Plymouth, Michigan

Essential Duties and Responsibilities include supporting plant operations with the following:

System Design & Programming

Design and program PLC/HMI systems for foam manufacturing equipment

Develop and maintain ladder logic, function block diagrams, and structured text programs

Create operator interface screens and SCADA systems

Process Control & Optimization

Monitor and optimize foam production processes including mixing, curing, and cutting operations

Implement process control strategies for temperature, pressure, and chemical dosing systems

Troubleshoot control system issues to minimize production downtime

Maintenance & Support

Provide technical support for production equipment and automation systems

Develop and support preventive maintenance procedures for control panels, sensors, and instrumentation

Document control system modifications and maintain as-built drawings

Maintain archive of PLC programs throughout facility.

Project Management

Lead automation upgrade projects from concept to commissioning

Coordinate with equipment vendors and contractors

Manage project budgets and timelines

Education and Experience:

Bachelor's Degree in Electrical or Controls Engineering or equivalent

Minimum 5 years of industry experience – automotive interiors experience preferred

Experience installing automated equipment such as assembly lines and robot assembly cells preferred.

Experience with seating products desired

Technical Skills: PLC programming and troubleshooting

HMI programming

Robot cell design and programming

Industrial networking

System specification, ordering, run-off, installation and qualification

Capital equipment budget planning and tracking

Strong computer skills with ability to quickly learn new software/applications

Ability to generate, update and maintain machinery electrical schematics and machinery diagrams in conventional (2D) CAD software

Analytical problem solving

Knowledge of industrial networks (Ethernet/IP, Profibus, Modbus)

Understanding of electrical codes and safety standards (NEC, NFPA 79)
