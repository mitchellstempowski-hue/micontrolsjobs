Senior Controls Engineer

Steelcase · Grand Rapids, Michigan

We're seeking a Controls Engineer to support the production equipment building and installation processes by conceptualizing, designing, programming, and troubleshooting electrical control systems!

Helping You Thrive By:

Offering competitive wages and benefits, that support your life both in and out of work

Creating continuous learning opportunities to help you grow and upskill.

Fostering a culture of inclusion where employees feel seen, heard and valued - and living it out every day.

Empowering you to make a meaningful impact on people and the planet through your work and Steelcase's ongoing commitment.

You'll Support Meaningful Work By:

Supporting the production equipment building and installation processes by conceptualizing, designing, programming, and troubleshooting electrical control systems. Typical work includes managing multiple projects involving large automated systems and multi-processor equipment.

Serving as a technical training resource for plant and corporate electricians.

Being a leader to the other engineers, assist the manager with department administration, and help improve department efficiencies.

Conceptualizing the electrical control system solution that is necessary for a given project, develop a design, and program and debug the system using knowledge of codes and standards including but not limited to NEC, NFPA, OSHA, MIOSHA, ANSI, etc.

Troubleshooting new and existing electrical control systems.

Managing multiple large projects. Assisting in the scheduling and coordination of any internal or external resources needed to complete a project, estimate the project cost, document the project, and follow up with the customer to ensure satisfaction. Seeking out cost reduction opportunities for existing processes.

Clarifying design issues and direct Machine Build Electricians, Plant Maintenance Electricians, Machine Builders, and Tooling Personnel.

Making electrical control recommendations to other disciplines, including Safety, Manufacturing Engineering, Facilities and Production.

Researching and evaluating new technology for electrical controls systems, including machine controls, robotics, and industrial applications of personal computers. Also serving as a technical resource for corporate wide electrical personnel.

Promoting and authoring the Corporate Electrical Controls Specification for automated equipment standards. Lead technical training seminars, classes, and programs for electrical personnel.

Who You Are:

Required Skills and Competencies

B.S. in Electrical Engineering or equivalent relevant experience

3 years Electrical Controls experience

Preferred Skills and Competencies

5 + years of experience in controls and automation engineering

Strong programming skills beyond Ladder Logic and ability to program multi-processor equipment.
