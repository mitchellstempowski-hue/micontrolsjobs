Jr Automation Engineer

Albireo Energy · Detroit, Michigan

Pay: $80,000 - $110,000 · Type: Full-time

Albireo Energy is seeking a Controls / Automation Engineer to support mission-critical data center infrastructure. The role focuses on developing, deploying, and maintaining industrial control systems in high-availability environments, using PLC programming, SCADA development, and system integration. The ideal candidate has experience with Allen-Bradley (Rockwell) PLCs, Ignition SCADA, and industrial networking, and can work in fast-paced, uptime-sensitive environments.

Responsibilities include developing, programming, and maintaining PLC control logic primarily on Allen-Bradley / Rockwell Automation platforms, including ControlLogix, CompactLogix, MicroLogix, Micro800, and Studio 5000; designing and configuring Ignition SCADA applications, including HMI screens, alarms, trending, and data visualization; integrating PLCs, SCADA systems, and third-party devices using protocols such as Modbus TCP/IP, BACnet/IP, and OPC UA; and supporting Electrical Power Monitoring Systems (EPMS) and other critical infrastructure systems in data centers.
