Sr. Manager, US Process Automation Lead

Pfizer · Kalamazoo, Michigan

Pay: $124,400 - $201,400 per year

Use Your Power for Purpose

The US Automation Lead, GH&B Global Engineering will be a member of the GH&B GE PCT Team and be responsible for supporting six US sites for all aspects of automation.

The role will also involve coordination with the GH&B GE International PCT to support 6 international sites as needed. Occasional support may be needed throughout the Pfizer network at internal and external manufacturing sites.

What You Will Achieve

Provide technical automation support and oversight for GH&B US manufacturing sites.

Establish and facilitate management of GH&B metrics, KPIs and operational reviews across the GE PCT, working with each site to develop and maintain a best in class automation program.

Drive Standardization & optimize GH&B automation solutions and automation master planning at sites.

Support automation projects that may include manufacturing equipment such as: filling, packaging, particle monitoring, BMS, batch operations, miscellaneous equipment.

Drive site automation master planning and long term strategy on automation programs.

Review automation scope and execution plan of capital investments and contribute to project team execution strategy and resource planning for large projects.

Oversee large master service agreements and negotiate with large automation partners.

Here Is What You Need (Minimum Requirements)

Applicant must have a bachelor's degree with at least 6 years of experience; OR a master's degree with at least 5 years of experience; OR a PhD with 1+ years of experience

Experience in production, engineering or process automation

At site experience in a pharmaceutical manufacturing environment

Knowledge of Rockwell Automation (PLC & SCADA) Systems and AMPS solution platforms.

Strong project management, change agility and problem-solving capabilities.

Proven ability to define and lead projects, track and execute with quality and timeliness on complex programs of work.
