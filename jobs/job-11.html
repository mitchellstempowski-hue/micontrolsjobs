Automation Technician

CCS Havel · Saginaw, Michigan

Pay: $18.00 - $45.00 per hour

About Us: We offer mechanical and electrical contracting, specialized controls and automation systems, proven energy efficiency services, and ongoing maintenance and support. We work in all commercial, institutional, and industrial market segments, pursuing plan/specification projects, owner-direct relationships, and design-build affiliations.

Job Summary: Havel is seeking a Controls & Automation Technician to install hardware design and software programming, documenting processes for the Building Management Control Direct Digital Control Systems.

Essential Duties & Responsibilities:

Complete typical installation hardware design and software programming using established standards as required

Provide detailed information to communicate design and operation to customers and subcontractors

Responsible for graphics building

Check out of HVAC control systems and wiring

Complete the loading, device verification, and commissioning of all system controllers as required

Document processes for the Building Management Control Direct Digital Control Systems

Perform site-specific training for owner/operator on the total system in full of assistance

Develop long-term relationships with contractors, clients, consultants, and subcontractors

Attend job progress meetings as required

Adhere to safety standards

Must be able to stoop, kneel, and climb ladders when needed

Other duties assigned

Qualifications:

Minimum of a technical Associates Degree with 1-2 years of field experience or 3 years of equivalent related experience in the HVAC industry

Must have relevant technical hands on systems design and commissioning of digital HVAC controls systems

Strong customer and communication skills to a non-technical audience

Must have experience with Microsoft Word and Excel

Niagara AX certified preferred

Must work in a team environment

Regular and reliable attendance, including the ability to work extended hours and weekends as required.

Compensation Range: $18.00 - $45.00 per hour
