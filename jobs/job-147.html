Automation Technician

Transform Automotive · Shelby Charter Township, Michigan

Type: Full-time

Transform Automotive is hiring a Automation Technician in Shelby Twp, MI. This is a full-time position.

About the role: As an Automation Technician, you’ll support the setup, troubleshooting, and operation of robotic and automated equipment throughout our manufacturing facility. You’ll work alongside Maintenance Technicians to resolve equipment issues, perform preventive maintenance, support new processes, and optimize automation for production and quality. This role offers hands-on experience with robotics and manufacturing automation while you continue building your technical skills.

What you'll do: Assist with the setup, operation, and troubleshooting of robotic and automated equipment. Monitor production equipment for robotic faults and perform basic troubleshooting, resets, and restarts. Support preventive maintenance activities to keep robotics and automation equipment operating effectively. Assist with robotic start-ups, shutdowns, new process implementation, job sampling, and data collection.

What you'll need to succeed: Experience working in a manufacturing environment. Experience working with Nachi and FANUC robots. Basic PLC knowledge, including Allen-Bradley and Logix software.
