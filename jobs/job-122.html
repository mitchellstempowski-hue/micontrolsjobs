Controls Engineer

Webasto · Plymouth, Michigan

Type: Permanent

Controls Engineer — Location: Plymouth, MI, US. Entry Level: Professionals (3+ years). Function: Production & Operations. Type of Contract: Permanent. Job ID: 31550.

About us: The Webasto Group is one of the 100 largest suppliers to the automotive industry. With our innovative sliding and panoramic sunroofs, convertible roofs, and groundbreaking heating, cooling, battery, and charging systems, we constantly create added value for the mobility sector.

Job Responsibilities:

Provide programming and electronic support for Manufacturing Engineering and Maintenance to support production. This includes machine and process troubleshooting

Support controls integration of quality and equipment related fail safes

Develop and modify PLC and Robotic Programs including Siemens Software TIA Portal, WinCC, Step 7 V5.6

Develop and modify Data Acquisition Systems

Proposes and implement electrical control systems that optimize machine and process performance

Ensure equipment compliance with OSHA, NEC, NFPA and other applicable standards

Train maintenance and manufacturing personnel on control systems

Create and update electrical drawings for equipment and facilities power distribution

Shut down any equipment which is deemed a safety hazard

Approves new and modified equipment and equipment electrical design changes

Authorize repair work

Education and Experience:

Bachelors Degree in Electrical Engineering, or equivalent level of experience.

3+ years Siemens PLC Programming experience in automotive/manufacturing, preferably TIA Portal experience

High Level programming language

Industrial electricity, safety circuits and devices, Ladder Logic, Operator Interface software

Excellent verbal, written, and interpersonal communication skills, Ability to work in a team oriented environment

Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and knowledge of databases

HMI Programming

Communications ( Profinet, Profibus, IO-Link)

Configuring/Programming of VFD's, Safety scanners, Barcode readers, DC torque controller/guns, vision cameras and AGV's
