Senior Controls Engineer

FANUC America · Rochester Hills, Michigan

Type: Full-time

The Senior Controls Engineer designs, develops, debugs, and delivers electrical controls hardware and software solutions for customers of FANUC America's Automated Systems Group (ASG). As a senior member of the engineering team, this role provides technical leadership on automation projects and leads controls engineering activities from concept through implementation, including PLC programming, controls specification development, system integration, testing, customer support, and startup. The role collaborates with cross-functional teams, mentors fellow engineers, and works directly with customers to develop solutions that meet technical, operational, and business requirements. It supports customer production and manufacturing objectives through automation systems and requires a customer-focused, service-first approach in a fast-paced environment. Requirements: Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering or a related field, or equivalent relevant experience. At least four years of experience designing and developing PLC applications using Siemens or Rockwell platforms. At least three years in a leadership role guiding teams and implementing engineering strategies. Experience with PLC programming, Siemens TIA Portal, Allen Bradley programming, HMI development, and electrical print design. Proven experience with controls architectures including EtherNet/IP, Profinet, Profibus, and Profisafe.
