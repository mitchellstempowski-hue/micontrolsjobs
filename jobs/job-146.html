Automation & Vision Engineering Technician (3rd Shift)

Perrigo · Allegan, Michigan

Shift: 3rd Shift

The Automation/Vision Engineering Technician plays a critical role in supporting manufacturing and packaging operations by maintaining and improving automation and vision systems. This position involves hands-on troubleshooting, system upgrades, and collaboration across departments to ensure reliable and efficient production processes.

Scope of the role: Provide technical support during third-shift operations, including emergency response and troubleshooting. Collaborate with engineering, maintenance, operations, and IT teams to resolve automation and vision system issues. Lead and support system upgrades to enhance reliability, efficiency, and safety. Participate in change management activities and maintain accurate documentation of system modifications. Review and validate equipment specifications to ensure compliance with site standards.

Preferred qualifications include a minimum 5 years of experience programming Allen-Bradley PLCs and HMIs (RSLogix500, Studio5000) and a minimum 5 years of experience with industrial vision systems (e.g., barcode scanning, label presence, OCR), with a solid understanding of Ethernet/IP communication, drives/servos, robotics, and control system design.
